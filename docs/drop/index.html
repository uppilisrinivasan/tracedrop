Drop: how it works and how to test it

Drop is TraceDrop's assistant. It turns each donation's free lab panel into a light health follow-up between donations, and keeps the donor's blood and its impact in view, all through a few short cards instead of a tracker.

Why Drop exists

People can donate blood every 90 days (men) or 120 days (women). That's too rare to keep anyone opening an app. But every donation comes with a free lab panel: BP, haemoglobin, sugar, cholesterol and more. Drop uses that:

Between donations

Drop follows up on what the results found: a goal, a free check-up, a check-in every two weeks, a reminder the donor asked for.

At each donation

The donation is the check-up. It measures progress and starts a new cycle.

All the time

Where their blood went and who it helped, without revealing the patient.

What Drop is not: a calorie or food tracker, or an app that pings all day. No meal logs, no streaks, no daily nagging. Drop sends few messages, merges them, and stays quiet when nothing matters. Every screen still works without Drop.

How Drop decides what to show

The rules always decide which cards exist and when. The AI only phrases them, so the same history always gives the same cards, and the demo behaves the same every time.

The rules

RuleWhat it does
Message budgetAt most 1 new card a day. Donors with a health goal: 2 a week plus a weekly report on Sunday afternoon. Healthy donors: 1 a week plus a monthly report.
PriorityWhen two arrive the same day: urgent → results → donating → check-in / Plan → goal reached → impact. Other blood used, circle news and activity only go into the report.
Outside the budgetUrgent cards; cards the donor asked for (slots after "Book a check-up"); follow-ups to something they did ("How did the check-up go?"); their own reminders.
Quiet hoursNothing new from 9 pm to 8 am. Waits until morning. Exceptions: urgent cards and the donor's own reminders.
Snooze⋯ or "Not now": 1 hour, tonight (7 pm), tomorrow, next week, or hide until the app is opened again. Snoozed 3 times → folds into the next report.
Urgent cardsAlways first, can't be hidden, can only wait 1 hour, never expire.
UnansweredA card left for 7 days folds into the next report ("Still waiting: …" with a button to answer it).
ImpactOnly the first unit from a donation that reaches a patient gets its own card; the rest go in the report and the story.
RemindersOnly ones the donor asked for. Taken / Skipped. Ignored → "no answer" next day, no nagging, no streaks.
MemoryPreferences and life context only (days that suit, work, area, language, food). Never health facts, religion, caste, income, family health or anything about recipients. Max 20, deletable, a newer one replaces a contradicting one.
BookingsBooked check-ups and donations show under "Coming up" on Home with Change and Cancel, any time. Undo on a receipt only lasts until time moves on.
CycleThe conversation is per donation cycle; the Plan and memory carry over.

Where the AI comes in

Card wording

Gemini rewrites up to 3 open cards in the donor's language. If it drops any number, the card keeps the rule wording. If the card's content changes, it is rewritten.

Drop's chat

"Ask Drop" answers in two short sentences plus at most one card: a chart, check-up slots or a reminder. It reads its memory and the Plan every turn.

Lab reports

Reads a photo of a lab report (Meera's sample report) and the donor confirms before saving.

With the AI switched off, everything still works: cards use rule wording and the chat answers common questions (results, BP, impact, donating, cover, check-up, memory) from rules.

Run it locally

git checkout feature/drop-home
./run.sh              # first run installs packages; then open http://localhost:5173

In the repo's .env:

SettingUse
AUTH_DISABLED=trueSkips Google sign-in locally.
GOOGLE_CLOUD_PROJECTYour project, for Gemini on Vertex AI. Leave empty to test with the AI off.
LLM_RATE_LIMIT_TOKENS_PER_DAYDaily AI budget (50,000 by default). A long test session can use it up; raise it locally if cards fall back to rule wording.

For the AI on a laptop, sign in once: gcloud auth application-default login.

Good to know: Drop's state is kept in the backend's memory. Restarting the backend (any backend code change does this with ./run.sh) puts the demo back to Sat 10 Oct. Use Reset on the demo bar to start a test cleanly.
