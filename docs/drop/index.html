Drop: how it works and how to test it

Drop is Pratidaan's assistant. It tells donors where their blood went, turns each donation's free lab panel into a light health follow-up between donations, and shows them the way back to donating, all through a few short cards instead of a tracker.

The whole product is described in docs/02-product.md and the build in docs/03-architecture.md. This page is for testing Drop.

Why Drop exists

People can donate blood every 90 days (men) or 120 days (women). That's too rare to keep anyone opening an app. But every donation comes with a free lab panel: BP, haemoglobin, sugar, cholesterol and more. Drop uses that:

Between donations

Drop follows up on what the results found: a Goal with one target and a few steps, a check-up, a check-in every two weeks, a reminder the donor asked for.

At each donation

The donation is the check-up. It measures progress and starts a new cycle.

All the time

Where their blood went and who it helped, without revealing the patient.

What Drop is not: a calorie or food tracker, or an app that pings all day. No meal logs, no streaks, no daily nagging. Drop sends few messages, merges them, and stays quiet when nothing matters. Every screen still works without Drop.

The New donor's Intro, Choices and Your data

The Intro is Drop's scripted first conversation with a new user, in Home's chat. It runs with the AI off, takes about a minute, and every step can be skipped. Only the New donor gets it; every other donor has done it. Its progress, language and Choices belong to the New donor and go back to the start with Reset.

Questions and Skip

Typed questions go to the AI and the answer appears in the thread, then "Carry on: {step}". Skip asks to confirm and keeps the defaults. Booking from Home's button mid-Intro opens the Donate screen.

Coming back

Other screens in the same visit keep the thread. A reload or new sign-in folds what was done into "Done so far" and Drop says "Welcome back". Finished or skipped, it folds into one line, "Intro · finished 10 Oct · Replay", with the questions you asked.

Replay

"Replay intro" in the account menu, only when viewing the New donor. It starts at step 1 with your earlier answers filled in and saves only what you change; skipping a replay changes nothing.

Choices are the donor's three sharing switches. The insurer switch shows its live effect ("On: your cover is ₹6 lakh. Off: it stays at ₹5 lakh") and says no insurer receives anything in the demo; with the leaderboard switch off you still see yourself ("Only you see yourself here"); with the totals switch off you are left out of every Circle total and the leaderboard ("1 member not counted"). They can be changed in the Intro, Your data, Health cover and Community: all the same switches.

Your data (account menu) lists everything kept about you: with your Google account (profile, sign-in history, donation preferences, bookings), in your own Google account (Calendar events, Gmail, access), with the demo donor (Choices, memories, readings, values read from lab reports), Gemini, server logs and this browser. Each item has a Delete button with a 6-second Undo, or says when it deletes itself. Delete my account removes the profile, sign-ins, preferences and bookings (optionally their Calendar events), clears the browser and signs you out; type DELETE to confirm. With sign-in off it clears the in-memory copies. The privacy policy (/privacy) is a mock that says the same things.

Goals, Habits and Chats

A Goal is one health problem the donor is working on, made from their results by fixed rules (backend/src/domain/goals.js). Neither the AI nor the donor sets grouping, the target or the order.

RuleWhat it does
GroupsLifestyle (BP, cholesterol, blood sugar, weight) and iron (haemoglobin, ferritin). A group makes a Goal only with a moderate or worse finding; its watch findings then join it. Watch-only groups and a raised liver enzyme go under Keep an eye on.
NameWhat it covers, most serious first, up to three: "BP, blood sugar and cholesterol", "Haemoglobin and iron".
TargetOne, set by the most serious finding: BP under 130/80; HbA1c under 5.7%; LDL under 130 and triglycerides under 150; Hb 12.5 (women) or 13.0 (men) with ferritin 15; diabetes range: a doctor's check within 30 days.
OrderUrgent, high, moderate; on a tie, the one that stops donation, then the older. At most two are active; the rest are Up next.
StatesActive, Up next, done (back to watch or better), stopped. An urgent Goal can't be stopped. A done Goal reopens if its findings come back.
Write-up"Why it matters" and 2–3 steps tagged Movement, Food, See a doctor or Add a reading, each citing a protocol or guidance from data/ontology. Rules, not AI, written once when the Goal is made or changes. An urgent BP Goal has no Movement step.

My health shows Habits (Movement, Food, Mind), This week, up to two Goal cards, Up next, the Health score with Your Chats with Drop, Keep an eye on, From your doctor (not built), Check-ups, the lab report reader, Trends and Readings. A Goal card has its trend in words, a chart against the target line (your readings are hollow points), Open and Add a reading.

Chats. On Home, Drop's chat is the General Chat. Elsewhere, Ask Drop opens a side sheet with chips for General, each Goal, Movement, Food, Mind and Past Cycles. A Goal's Chat pins where you are, the target and this week. Its ⋯ menu has Add a reading, Book a check-up, Stop this Goal (not for an urgent Goal), What Drop remembers, and Simplify my plan (disabled until Week plans). Done and stopped Goals' Chats are read-only; a stopped Goal has Start again. In a Goal's Chat, Drop suggests only that Goal's steps and says saving a Week plan is coming. Past Cycles shows This cycle (live) server-side turns as you chat (guest or signed-in, per persona) and read-only Earlier cycles after Reset on the demo bar; demo personas include a sample earlier cycle. History is in-memory on Cloud Run (not Firestore). Vertex Agent Sessions mirror turns separately when configured. Week plans and Your day are not built yet.

Stories and Badges

A Story is a few tap-through slides about one part of a donation reaching a patient, or plasma becoming medicines. Drop's impact Card plays it once; My impact replays it from Watch the story on any eligible part. Patients' details appear only when at least 5 patients share them; anything held back is left out and a line says "Some details stay private so no one can be identified." The last slide is a postcard image with Download image, Copy image and Share… (where the browser can share files). The weekly report and the Intro's Story can't be shared.

Badges (8: First patient, 5 patients, 10 patients, Regular donor, Nothing wasted, All five parts, Plasma to medicine, Helped a child) are worked out from the donor's own data. My impact shows the shelf ("Badges · n of 8"): earned ones in gold with the month, unearned ones with a progress ring and "· 8 so far". Home shows four. Earning one never sends a Card: it shows in the next Story or report, on Drop's line on My impact, and with a New tag until opened. Full rules: Story and Badge spec.

How Drop decides what to show

The rules always decide which cards exist and when. The AI only phrases them, so the same history always gives the same cards, and the demo behaves the same every time.

The rules

RuleWhat it does
Message budgetAt most 1 new card a day. Donors with a health goal: 2 a week plus a weekly report on Sunday afternoon. Healthy donors: 1 a week plus a monthly report.
PriorityWhen two arrive the same day: urgent → results → donating → check-in → goal reached → impact. Other blood used and activity only go into the report.
Outside the budgetUrgent cards; cards the donor asked for (slots after "Book a check-up"); follow-ups to something they did ("How did the check-up go?"); their own reminders.
Quiet hoursNothing new from 9 pm to 8 am. Waits until morning. Exceptions: urgent cards and the donor's own reminders.
Snooze⋯ or "Not now": 1 hour, tonight (7 pm), tomorrow, next week, or hide until the app is opened again. Snoozed 3 times → folds into the next report.
Urgent cardsAlways first, can't be hidden, can only wait 1 hour, never expire.
UnansweredA card left for 7 days folds into the next report ("Still waiting: …" with a button to answer it).
ImpactOnly the first unit from a donation that reaches a patient gets its own card; the rest go in the report and the story.
RemindersOnly ones the donor asked for. Taken / Skipped. Ignored → "no answer" next day, no nagging, no streaks.
MemoryFrom chat, only a stated schedule or language preference ("Saturdays suit you", "Evenings suit you", "Hindi suits you"). Never health facts, medicines, religion, caste, income, family health or anything about recipients. Seeded personas also start with a little life context. Max 20, deletable, a newer one replaces a contradicting one.
Bookings"Try demo donation booking" (or "Book my first donation") opens the booking steps inside Drop's chat on Home and ends with a check-in pass. Booked check-ups and donations show under "Coming up" on Home with Change and Cancel, any time. Undo on a receipt only lasts until time moves on.
CycleThe conversation is per donation cycle; the Plan, Goals and memory carry over.

Where the AI comes in

Card wording

Gemini is asked to rephrase up to 3 open cards, but because cards carry triage and eligibility, a rewrite is used only if it matches the rule wording word for word. In practice cards keep their rule wording; only the slots card has a Hindi version.

Drop's chat

On Home the chat sits under Drop's cards; on other screens "Ask Drop" opens the side sheet. An agent with 18 tools answers in two short sentences plus at most one card: a chart, check-up slots or a reminder. It reads its memory, the Plan and each Goal's steps every turn, knows which Chat it is in, saves BP readings, and can switch its language when asked. Guardrails: regex blocks emergencies and diagnosis asks; every call gets scope rules (no essays/homework); replies are sanitized for prescription wording.

Lab reports

Reads a photo of a lab report (Meera's sample report) and the donor confirms before saving.

The Intro

Translates each step's words and buttons the first time they are shown in a language, keeping every number. The steps themselves are scripted.

With the AI switched off, everything still works: cards use rule wording and the chat answers common questions (results, BP, iron, impact, donating, cover, check-up, memory, language, the Intro step) from rules, marked "AI offline: answered by rules". Goal write-ups are rules either way.

Run it locally

git checkout main
./run.sh              # first run installs packages; then open http://localhost:5173

In the repo's .env:

SettingUse
AUTH_DISABLED=true, NODE_ENV=developmentSkips Google sign-in locally. On the live site, use Continue as guest instead.
GOOGLE_CLOUD_PROJECTYour project, for Gemini on Vertex AI. Leave empty to test with the AI off.
LLM_RATE_LIMIT_TOKENS_PER_DAYDaily AI budget (50,000 by default). A long test session can use it up; raise it locally if cards fall back to rule wording.

For the AI on a laptop, sign in once: gcloud auth application-default login.

Good to know: Drop's state is kept in the backend's memory. Restarting the backend (any backend code change does this with ./run.sh) puts the demo back to Sat 10 Oct. Use Reset on the demo bar to start a test cleanly.
