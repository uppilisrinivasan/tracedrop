# Demo video script (under 3:00)

**Rule:** the first minute shows the product doing real work, including Gemini. Judges see the video first, then open the live link.

**Format:** a screen recording of the deployed prototype at 1440 px, with an English voice-over. Kannada and Hindi on screen get English subtitles. Upload as **public** on YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive, and keep a backup copy on Drive.

**Before recording:**
- Open the live app.
- Sign in, or **Continue as guest**.
- Click **Reset** on the demo bar.
- Pick **Arjun Rao** in **Viewing as**.
- Check the AI console (`/#ai`) says "Verified", so Gemini is live. If it isn't, the chat still answers from rules, but the Kannada answer and the lab-report beats need Gemini.

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p or higher, and zoom the browser so the text is at least 16 px.
- Keep each number on screen for at least 2 seconds.
- If Gemini is slow, cut the wait. Don't fake a reply.
- Subtitle every non-English line.
- Show the "Demo" and "Illustrative" labels; don't crop them out.
- Do the timing rehearsal from **Reset** each time; the demo clock is shared.
- Upload, then open the link in an incognito window to confirm it is public.
