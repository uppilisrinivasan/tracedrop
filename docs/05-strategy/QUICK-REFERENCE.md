- **Institutional outcome:** Sustainable funding from seeing consumer demand

### Layer 3: Ecosystem Orchestration
- Everyone benefits when the donor succeeds
- No competition between stakeholders
- All incentives aligned
- **System outcome:** Self-sustaining at scale
