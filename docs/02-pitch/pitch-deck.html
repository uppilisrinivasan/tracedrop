Why nobody has fixed it

Results alone don't work. Follow-up does, and it needs a person.

Japan, since 1982

Results returned

The Red Cross sends donors 15 blood values through the Love Blood app.

Reference ranges are "not normal/abnormal judgements", and there's no next step.

Taiwan, 2025

Screening added

HbA1c and cholesterol tests for donors over 40.

42.7% didn't know they'd been screened. The study found "limited benefits… without additional interventions".

India, 2026

AI explainers exist

Aarogya Setu 2.0 "Smart Reports", AI trends, 92 crore ABHA IDs.

Plenty of records and trends, but nobody acts on them. Only 491 labs are connected to ABDM, against 27,283 hospitals.

The missing link is a navigator.

It has to know your history, explain why a finding matters to you in your language, book the next step and keep following up until it's done. That's what executives get from a physician and a care coordinator.

Every other piece already exists and much of it is free: Ayushman Arogya Mandir health centres, eSanjeevani (50 crore teleconsultations), free hepatitis treatment under NVHCP, and ABHA records. Nothing carries the donor to them.

TraceDrop

A navigator for every donor, with the blood centre in charge

Gemini · multimodal

1. Capture

Reads a register page, a lab-report photo or a PDF from any lab and writes one standard FHIR record. No integration needed.

Gemini · long context

2. Understand

Reasons over the donor's whole history using ICMR protocols. Safety thresholds stay as fixed rules.

Gemini · Indian languages

3. Explain

Explains why it matters to them, in their language, and answers "but I have no symptoms" with their own trend.

ADK agent · tools

4. Act

Books free care at an Ayushman Arogya Mandir, on eSanjeevani or for a recheck, then follows up until it's done.

Doctor approves

Every plan that changes care waits for the centre's doctor, using ADK tool confirmation. Approvals can be done in batches, in seconds.

Counsellor discloses

Infection results never appear in any agent message. The agent only books "a confidential conversation", and follows up afterwards.

Red flags skip the AI

BP ≥180/120, or symptoms such as chest pain or breathlessness, trigger fixed urgent templates (ICMR 2026). No model judgement is involved.

It never diagnoses or prescribes (Telemedicine Practice Guidelines §5.4). It never offers anything for donating (D&C Rule 122EA). Care never depends on donating.

Impact and scale

It routes to care the state already pays for. No new tests, no new budget.

Who benefits

  • Donors: silent conditions found and followed up
  • Women: 57% are anaemic, and they're the most often deferred
  • People with hepatitis: curable, free treatment under NVHCP
  • Blood centres: they meet their counselling duties, and cleared donors come back
  • Patients: more regular donors

Who pays (donors never do)

  • Blood centres and SBTCs: counselling and NVHCP linkage duties
  • Employers: annual check-ups under the OSH Code (in force Nov 2025)
  • Insurers: wellness programmes allowed by IRDAI, with consent

Path to scale

  • 3 Bengaluru centres → every finding type → every donor
  • SBTCs and NHM; ABHA and eSanjeevani connection
  • JAPAC: Japan returns results and Taiwan screens, but neither navigates donors to care

Built to be trusted

Synthetic data in the prototype · thresholds from ICMR 2026 and WHO · the doctor approves and the counsellor discloses · DPDP consent, and employers see aggregates only · a deployment would register as CDSCO medical device software and follow ICMR's 2023 AI ethics guidelines.
